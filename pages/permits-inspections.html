Readiness

Permits applied/granted and inspections passed/failed per phase. Failing an inspection blocks that schedule phase.

What the AHJ typically wants to see

  • Footing inspection before concrete (foundation widths + soil + frost).
  • Rough plumbing + pressure test before cover; electrical rough before insulation.
  • Framing + roofing dry-in, then insulation, then final for occupancy.
