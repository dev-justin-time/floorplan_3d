Glossary

Photometry, controls, code terms and the electrical words on the schedule.

4 Key concepts

Lux (lx) & Lumens (lm)

A lumen is the total quantity of visible light emitted from a source. One lux is equal to one lumen per square meter (1 lx = 1 lm/m²), representing illuminance incident on a surface.

Correlated Colour Temperature (CCT)

Characterisation of the spectral colour appearance of white light, measured in Kelvins (K). Lower ratings (2700K) exhibit warm reddish/amber tones; higher ratings (4000K+) appear crisp neutral to icy blue.

LPD (Lighting Power Density)

The total connected lighting electrical load divided by the interior area (Watts per square meter or W/m²). Key benchmark in ASHRAE 90.1 and IECC energy codes.

Uniformity Ratio (Emin / Eavg)

The minimum illuminance divided by the average illuminance across the calculation grid. Higher values (>0.6) signify smooth, glare-free light distribution without stark shadows.
