    Formulas · steep access

    1. Slope θ = atan(h ÷ G). Alternating tread ≤ 51°, ships ladder 50–70°, industrial 30–50°.
    2. Risers ceil(rise ÷ maxRiser): 9.5 in alternating, 10 in ships, 9.5 in OSHA; treads risers − 1.
    3. Going from the space: G = run ÷ treads must clear 8.5 in alternating / 5 in ships / 9.5 in OSHA.
    4. Half-treads alternate either side of the centre stringer, so each foot lands on a full tread at half the going.
    5. Handrails both sides (IBC 1011.14/1011.16); steep devices are occasional access only, never an accessible route.
