Gap policy

  • Filler 3–150 mm to close each end; wider needs a filler cabinet.
  • Air gap 25 mm at a refrigerator, 20 mm at a tall pantry.
  • Landing 600 mm beside the sink, 380 mm beside the range.
  • Aisle 1.07 m minimum, 1.2 m preferred between runs.
