Half turn · 180°

Drawn as a 180° winder turn. Prefer a landing? Split the rise in half — each flight climbs rise ÷ 2 with a landing ≥ flight width deep between them.

    Formulas · half turn

    1. Risers ceil(rise ÷ maxRiser); h = rise ÷ risers; treads risers − 1 spread over sweep = π.
    2. Angle per winder α = π ÷ treads; walkline tread Tw = (innerR + 12 in) × α clears the winder minimum.
    3. Inside tread Ti = innerR × α is the pinch point — the well width sets it. Enlarge the well when it fails.
    4. Two-flight landing option: each flight rise ÷ 2, flight run treads_half × Tw, landing ≥ width; total length run + landing.
    5. IBC curved rule: innerR ≥ 2 × width on commercial stairs.
