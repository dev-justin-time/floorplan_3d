    Formulas · quarter turn

    1. Risers ceil(rise ÷ maxRiser), step height h = rise ÷ risers, treads risers − 1.
    2. Walkline radius Rw = innerR + 12 in (code walkline offset from the narrow side).
    3. Angle per winder α = (π/2) ÷ treads; walkline tread Tw = Rw × α must clear the winder minimum (10 in IRC / 11 in IBC).
    4. Inside tread Ti = innerR × α must clear 6 in (IRC) / 10 in (IBC) — widen the turn when this fails.
    5. Comfort 2h + Tw → 24–25 in; footprint is the swept annular sector, not the run × depth rectangle.
