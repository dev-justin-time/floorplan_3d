Spiral · full 360° turn

Spirals are limited as a means of egress (IBC 1011.10) — a dwelling or small occupancy, not a primary exit.

    Formulas · spiral

    1. Risers ceil(rise ÷ 9.5 in); h = rise ÷ risers; treads risers − 1 over one full turn sweep = 2π.
    2. Walkline radius Rw = columnR + 12 in; angle per tread α = 2π ÷ treads.
    3. Walkline tread Tw = Rw × α ≥ 7.5 in (IRC) — the diameter check. Solve backwards: D ≥ 2 × (Tw·treads ÷ 2π + 12 in − columnR …), in practice widen D until Tw clears.
    4. Clear width outerR − columnR ≥ 26 in.
    5. Footprint is the D × D circle; headroom 78 in minimum on spirals.
